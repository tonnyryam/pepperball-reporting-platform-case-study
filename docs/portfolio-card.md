## PepperBall reporting reliability

**Role:** Forward Deployed Engineer Intern, May 2026-August 2026
**Focus:** Python, Azure, PostgreSQL, SharePoint, Microsoft Graph, deterministic Excel reporting, observability, and release engineering

Owned discovery through delivery of a manufacturing reporting platform that prepares Shift Drafts and daily, weekly, and downtime reports for human review. Built a deterministic one-writer core with durable source-to-output accounting, bounded recovery, exact readback, and rollback. Added machine-event intake, notifications, dashboard visibility, and a functional read-only Reporting Assistant as extensions; documented broader model-backed and advisory capabilities as future work.
