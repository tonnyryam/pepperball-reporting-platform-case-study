# Public claim boundaries

| Claim area | Safe public statement | Do not claim without new approval or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reliability | Equivalent retries were verified as no-ops and ambiguous accepted writes settled by readback. | The system can recover from every failure. |
| Correctness | Deterministic structure, stored values, lineage, semantics, and readback were tested. | Blanket report or business-data accuracy. |
| Operations | One-writer handoffs and exact rollback were exercised and observed. | Zero risk or guaranteed continuous availability. |
| Repair | Technical gaps can converge through bounded repair while terminal holds remain protected. | Missing business facts can be reconstructed automatically. |
| Impact | The work reduced classes of stale, duplicate, and ambiguous output failure. | Realized savings, adoption, productivity, or causal financial impact. |
| Scope | A manufacturing reporting platform was hardened across ingestion, workbooks, publication, and recovery. | Private topology, customer content, account identities, or exact production paths. |
| Delivery | Report-specific distribution is implemented, enabled, and end-to-end tested in a controlled test, including attachment/link delivery, audit updates, and same-file duplicate suppression. | Successful production delivery has been observed for the next real publication from all three report families. |
| Reporting core | Shift Drafts and daily, weekly, and downtime reporting are delivered with controlled ingestion, reconciliation, validation, publication, readback, recovery, and rollback. | Every possible operational input or business exception is automated. |
| Machine visibility | QR/Form machine-event intake, notifications, and a SharePoint dashboard were delivered as an additional initiative. | Modeled production units or margin were realized or caused by the system. |
| Reporting Assistant | A functional read-only extension answers bounded queries using source-grounded retrieval, structured responses, validated citations, diagnostics, evidence validation, and deterministic refusal. | The assistant writes reports, makes operational decisions, or has completed broader model-backed reasoning, document knowledge, recurring artifacts, or production advisory work. |
| ROI | 30 hours/week, 1,560 hours/year, about $52K labor-capacity value, and machine-visibility/retrieval scenarios are qualified estimates or models. | Add separate value pools, claim payback, or present modeled value as booked savings. |

## Language rules

- Say `verified`, `observed`, or `tested` only for the corresponding evidence.
- Distinguish deployed behavior from local prototypes and planned work.
- Treat missing as missing, never as zero.
- Use synthetic values in every screenshot and demonstration.
- Do not translate coverage, similarity, or classification metrics into blanket
  accuracy.
- Do not use the reviewed production-count schema-conformance result in public materials.
